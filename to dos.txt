[MUST] main.py:~503 (_start_ipc_listener) & app_icon.py:_notify — you call self.after(0, …) / self.after(1, …) from background threads. Tkinter is not thread-safe; Tcl only guarantees this on the creating thread (this is exactly the "cross-thread Tk calls are undefined (Tcl_NOTMAIN)" concern your own comment mentions, yet both sites do it anyway). It happens to work often because _tkinter shares one interpreter — but window state races with close()/tray rebuild and can corrupt Tcl or segfault on exit. → Replace with a thread-safe pattern: push into an queue.Queue from the workers, and have the main loop poll it (self.after(100, self._poll_queue)). One poller covers both the IPC socket and tray notifications.
